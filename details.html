Goku

Character Information

Full Name: Son Goku

Universe: Dragon Ball

Species: Saiyan

Role: Warrior and Protector

Power Level: Legendary

Backstory

Goku is a Saiyan warrior who was sent to Earth as a young child. After growing up on Earth, he became a skilled martial artist and dedicated his life to protecting his friends, family, and the planet.

Throughout his journey, Goku faces powerful opponents, discovers new abilities, and continues training to reach greater levels of strength.

Abilities

  • Super Saiyan transformations
  • Kamehameha energy attack
  • Instant Transmission
  • Advanced martial arts
  • Extreme physical strength and speed

Personality

Goku is cheerful, energetic, curious, and highly competitive. He enjoys training and challenging strong opponents. Despite his love of fighting, he deeply cares about his friends and is willing to protect others when they are in danger.

First Appearance

Goku first appeared in the manga Dragon Ball in 1984.
